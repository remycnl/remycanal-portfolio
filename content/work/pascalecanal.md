Pascale Canal is a French painter. She wanted a place online where people can see her paintings properly, buy the originals, and read the story behind each one. I designed and built the whole site.

![The gallery homepage](/works/pascalecanal/overview.webp "Homepage")

## The challenge

A gallery website has a strange problem: the more design you add, the more it competes with the art. Most of the work was deciding what to leave out.

There is a second problem. Buying an original painting online is not something many people have done, so the site also has to reassure.

## Approach

The interface stays quiet and lets the paintings carry the color. The brand is a **PC** monogram and a full wordmark, each in a light and a reversed version, so they sit equally well on pale and dark backgrounds.

The homepage is the gallery itself: a grid of paintings with filters and a search. No introduction page in front, because the work is the introduction.

## The pages

**Paintings.** Each painting comes with the story of how it was made, because that is what makes someone choose one canvas over another.

**How it works.** A short page that explains how ordering, payment and delivery work, written for someone who has never bought art online.

**Custom order.** Pascale also turns photographs into painted portraits and memories. This service is highlighted on the homepage and has its own page and form.

![The custom order page](/works/pascalecanal/custom-order.webp "Custom order: from a photo to a painting")

**About, events and contact.** A page about the artist, a page for her exhibitions and events, a contact page and an FAQ. A newsletter signup in the footer keeps visitors in touch with new work.

## Images

Paintings are large files, and texture matters. Images are resized on demand by the host, so every screen gets the right size and the detail is still there when you look closely.
