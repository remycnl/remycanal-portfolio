Vikl is an app that supports managers in the moments that are hard to face alone: a delicate piece of feedback, a disagreement to settle, a change to announce. The website has two jobs. It has to explain a new kind of product quickly, and it has to earn the trust of people who are about to type something sensitive into it.

![The Vikl homepage](/works/vikl/overview.webp "Homepage")

## The challenge

Two things made this site harder than a usual product page.

The first is trust. Telling a manager "an AI will help you with your team" raises an immediate question: who reads what I write? If that question isn't answered in the first scroll, nothing else matters.

The second is that Vikl has two audiences. An individual manager can subscribe on their own. A company wants to equip a whole team and needs to hear about security, dashboards and SSO. The same page can't speak to both.

## Structure

The site opens on a choice between **Manager** and **Entreprise**, each with its own story. It is available in French and Spanish.

The homepage follows the order of the questions a visitor asks. The promise and the app store buttons come first. Then three short steps: guided questions, concrete deliverables, preparation before acting. Right after, a row of privacy markers (GDPR, hosting in France, encryption, AI Act) sits on the first screens instead of hiding in the footer.

After that come the figures from a study of managers, a founder video with a short quote, the team and the values, a grid of concrete benefits, three pricing plans and an FAQ. The FAQ is written the way people actually ask: can an AI replace a coach, are my conversations confidential, how do you handle my data.

## Resources

Vikl publishes a lot: more than twenty articles, plus videos and podcasts. The resources section can be filtered by topic (feedback, conflict, isolation, leadership and so on), and each article is signed by a member of the team. The goal was to make the blog feel like part of the product, with a clear voice, rather than a pile of SEO pages.

## Build

The site is built with Nuxt. Images are optimized and served in modern formats, and the social preview is a short looping video of the product instead of a static card.
