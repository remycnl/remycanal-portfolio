## Small lessons last longer

This year was less about one big breakthrough and more about a collection of adjustments that made the work better.

The first is to prototype the risky part early. If an interaction depends on performance, touch input or an unfamiliar API, I want to know that before the rest of the page is built around it.

The second is to write down decisions. A short note about why a component behaves a certain way saves a surprising amount of time when the project changes a few weeks later.

The third is to leave space for a second pass. The first implementation answers whether something works. The second answers whether it feels right. Those are different questions and both deserve time.

## Progress is often quiet

Better naming, fewer dependencies, a clearer loading state or one less unnecessary abstraction may not look impressive in a changelog. They still change how a project feels to maintain.

I am trying to notice those improvements more. They are usually where the durable work happens.
