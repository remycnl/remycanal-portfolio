## Structure without losing freedom

Nuxt gives a project a useful shape from the beginning: pages, layouts, server routes, data fetching and build tooling all have a natural place. That structure removes a lot of setup work while leaving room for a very personal interface.

For a portfolio, that balance matters. The foundation should be dependable, but the final result should not feel like a template.

## Content belongs in the project

Nuxt Content makes the editorial side just as comfortable as the component side. Articles can live as readable Markdown, be validated with a schema and be queried like the rest of the application.

That means I can update a paragraph without touching the presentation, while still keeping dates, images and layout metadata predictable. It is a small distinction that makes a project much easier to grow.

## A good default for creative work

The tools disappear when they are doing their job well. Nuxt handles the boring but important parts so I can spend more attention on typography, interaction and the rhythm of a page.

That is why I keep coming back to it: it gives creative work a reliable home without trying to dictate what the work should look like.
