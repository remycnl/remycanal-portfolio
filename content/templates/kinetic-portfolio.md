## Let the work move

Kinetic Portfolio uses motion as a navigational tool. Transitions, offsets and changing scale create a sense of discovery without making the interface difficult to read.

It is made for visual identities, digital products and projects where the process matters as much as the final image.

## Built for momentum

The layout gives each project a distinct entrance while keeping the overall system consistent. Swap the imagery, tune the pacing and make the movement yours.
