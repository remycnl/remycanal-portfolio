## Make space for the work

Editorial Studio is built around a calm reading rhythm: strong typography, generous spacing and a layout that lets the content lead.

Use it as a starting point for a portfolio, a creative practice or a small collection of work. The structure stays quiet while the details give it character.

## A flexible foundation

The template keeps the visual system deliberately small. A restrained palette, clear type hierarchy and responsive composition make it easy to adapt without losing its point of view.
