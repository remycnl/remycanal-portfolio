Animaux d'à côté is a project built around the well-being of domestic animals: booking appointments with professionals, finding a lost pet, and helping each other out between neighbors.

The art direction had to reflect these three sides: the seriousness of care, the urgency of a search, and the warmth of a community. The palette, typography, illustrations and visual tone were designed to feel close and reassuring.
