J'aime le Morvan is a non-profit collective of Jeep enthusiasts who explore the trails of the Morvan and the wider Burgundy region.

The logo blends an off-road spirit with a deep attachment to the land. It stays simple and legible so it can be applied to stickers, clothing and all of the association's materials.
